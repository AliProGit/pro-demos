6. The risk

What the TODAY behaviour does to real cases, and why it grows with every new client.

394
cases on program-less rows
388 of them are not Invivyd cases but carry an Invivyd rep or Case Manager
2
programs with the flag off
YcanthRx and CorMedix: every save of a case with a provider in a legacy zip stamps a foreign rep
5
YcanthRx territories split between two FRMs at zip level
Richmond VA, Minneapolis MN, Dallas, Fort Worth, Houston. "Same territory" never predicts the FRM
54 / 442
Sales Rep contacts that even have a territory
the join between people and zips was mostly impossible
A rep from one client sees their name on another client's patient case. For a HIPAA-covered hub that is a reportable mistake waiting for the first email notification.
Territory names like "Dallas TX" repeat across clients. Each new client with US-city territories raises the chance that the text-only trigger picks the wrong client's rep.
The exposure is to the case record itself, not to patient visibility: reps do not log in. The risk is wrong routing, wrong emails, wrong reporting.
