Replacing a faulty product, from request to delivery

When a product arrives unusable, the program team asks the manufacturer to send a replacement. Built for any program; the first one to use it is the Delcath Hepzato kit, and nothing in the build names it. This page shows how that request is tracked in Salesforce as one case. You can run a request through the whole journey below and see what people do and what Salesforce does for them.

What is newThe request lives on the Free Goods case (Product Replacement type), uses the Free Goods stages exactly as they are today, and tracks the detail with to-dos.
Who is involvedProgram support staff, the patient's treating site (an enrolled patient), the manufacturer, and whoever ships the product.
How much workAbout 7.5 build days plus testing. Built with Salesforce settings only, no custom code, no new picklist values.

    The journey as a checklist

    The same journey, written out step by step. Each step says who acts, what Salesforce does on its own, and what has to be true before moving on.

    1. Request received Status: New

      Who actsA support associate opens the patient's record and starts a Product Replacement case. One case per request.
      Salesforce doesNames the case automatically (" - Product Replacement - patient name") and links the treating site.
      Must be trueThe patient is enrolled in the program, and the same site has not already had a replacement approved in the last 12 months. A missing HIPAA does not stop the case; it becomes a Missing Information item.
      Record type Free_Goods, Sub_Type__c = Product Replacement, Pro_Program__c = the program, AccountId = patient (Person Account), Treating_Site__c = facility. Subject by flow PAP_Free_Goods_Case_Subject. Created manually from the patient record (decision D5).

    2. Eligibility check Status: New

      Who actsThe support associate confirms the appeal was denied and the site is approved, then marks the case Eligible.
      Salesforce doesStamps who reviewed it and when.
      Must be trueNothing is checked by the system here. the first program’s history sits on the older system, so a person confirms it by hand.
      Fields: Appeal Denial Completed, Site Approved Verified (+ date), Eligibility Status = Eligible. Eligibility Reviewed By/Date are stamped by a before-save flow. The first program has zero Benefit Verification, Authorization or Appeal cases on the new architecture, so the condition cannot be derived from data.

    3. Collecting documents Incomplete Missing Information

      Who actsThe support associate gathers the five documents from the site (and the HIPAA authorization if it was missing) and ticks each one off as it arrives.
      Salesforce doesOpens the Missing Information checklist from the program's configuration: the five documents, plus "Valid HIPAA" when the patient's HIPAA is not valid. Clears each item when ticked. When the last one is in, it marks "Required Documentation Complete" itself.
      Must be trueAll items received before the case can move on. If they never arrive, the case is cancelled as "MI Not Received".
      Reuses the Missing Information framework: five new list values plus the existing "Valid HIPAA" item, one config row per program in PRO_Missing_Information_Config__mdt (Program_Key + Sub_Type = Product Replacement), one extra condition on PAP_Missing_Information_Check. Free_Goods_Requires_Valid_HIPAA exempts Product Replacement (decided 11/05).

    4. Packet complete, submitting Under Review Pending Determination To-do 1 open

      Who actsThe support associate moves the case to Under Review once the packet is complete.
      Salesforce doesCreates to-do 1: "Submit product replacement packet to manufacturer", due in 48 hours, listing the program’s manufacturer contacts (from the Program record). The open to-do is how everyone sees "we are submitting".
      Must be trueNo open document items.
      Validation rule gates Status = Under Review on an open MI child with items (same test the parent guard uses). New Task record type Manufacturer Review Request carries all four to-dos; Exception_Request_Task is deliberately not reused because it carries the Compassionate Care sign-off.

    5. Submitted, waiting Under Review Pending Determination To-do 2 open

      Who actsThe associate emails the packet to the manufacturer from Outlook (still manual in Phase 1), then completes to-do 1.
      Salesforce doesRecords "Submitted to Manufacturer", the date, sets the decision to Pending, and creates to-do 2: "Follow up with manufacturer on decision", due in 48 hours. Status and Sub Status do not move.
      Must be trueNothing extra. Overdue to-dos show in the existing task reports.

    6. Approved by the manufacturer, product on its way Under Review Pending Determination To-do 3, then 4

      Who actsThe associate records the approval with the purchase order (PO) number and date, then works to-do 3 (confirm shipment: courier, tracking) and to-do 4 (confirm delivery).
      Salesforce doesStamps the decision date, closes to-do 2, sets the shipment field to "Pending Shipment" and creates to-do 3. Completing to-do 3 sets it to "Shipped" and creates to-do 4. The case stays open; Status and Sub Status do not move.
      Must be truePO number and PO date are filled in.
      Flow on Pro_Manufacturer_Decision__c change. The case stays Under Review / Pending Determination because Approved is a closed status in this org and the July design says the case must stay open until the product arrives (D9 asks whether that should change). The existing Shipment Sub-Status field (Pending Shipment / Shipped / Shipment Complete) is the stamp the to-dos write.

    7. Delivered and closed Approved No Restrictions

      Who actsThe associate completes to-do 4 when the site confirms delivery.
      Salesforce doesStamps the completion date, closes the case as Approved and closes any leftover to-dos.
      Must be truePO number, tracking number and shipment date are all filled in.
      Flow sets Status = Approved, Sub Status = No Restrictions. PAP_Parent_Close_Cleanup closes open tasks. The PO date then starts the 12-month facility clock.

    8. Branch: manufacturer says no Denied Coverage Criteria

      Who actsThe associate records the denial and the manufacturer’s comments.
      Salesforce doesCloses the case as Denied / Coverage Criteria (the manufacturer's replacement criteria were not met) and closes to-do 2.
      Must be trueThe manufacturer's comments are filled in; they hold the reason.

    9. Branch: withdrawn or opened in error Cancelled

      Who actsThe associate cancels the case at any point before it closes.
      Salesforce doesUses the existing Cancelled behaviour of Free Goods.
      Must be trueA Sub Status is chosen (existing shared rule).

    Why "Approved" only appears at the very end

    In this Salesforce org, "Approved" locks the case as closed. The manufacturer approving the request is not the end of the work, because the kit still has to ship. So while the product is in transit the case stays "Under Review / Pending Determination" with an open to-do, and only becomes "Approved" once delivery is confirmed. Nothing in the shared Free Goods stages changes. Open question D9: the business may prefer "Approved" at the manufacturer's decision, like every other case type, with shipping tracked on the closed case.

    The to-dos that carry the detail

    Status and Sub Status stay on the Free Goods values we already have. Inside "Under Review" the detail is a to-do (a Salesforce task): one is open at a time, each one is created automatically when the previous one is completed, and completing it writes the result onto the case. This is how Free Goods already works for PAP; Product Replacement reuses the same mechanics.

    Naming clean-up that comes with this

    Several of these to-do types and texts still say "PAP" although Free Goods now has four sub-types: the task types "PAP Leadership Approval", "PAP Missing Information Follow-Up" and "PAP Renewal Assessment", the follow-up subject "FollowUp Task-1" and its description "missing PAP requirements", and the eligibility subject built from the sub-type. Proposal: rename the labels to "Leadership Approval", "Missing Information Follow-Up", "Renewal Assessment", make the follow-up subject and description use the sub-type, and keep the technical names for now (three flows reference them by name; an API rename is a separate clean-up story with a dependency check).

    Record types PAP_Leadership_Approval, PAP_Missing_Information_Follow_Up, PAP_Renewal_Assessment: label change only in this release. Their DeveloperNames are matched in PAP_Missing_Information_Check, MI_Parent_Change_Cleanup and PAP_Parent_Close_Cleanup. Subject "FollowUp Task-1" and the description "Follow up with the patient / provider for the missing PAP requirements" are hard-coded in PAP_Missing_Information_Check (formula fxTaskDesc) and should read the sub-type.

    Safety rules that keep the case honest

    Four rules apply only to Product Replacement cases, plus one duplicate check. Each blocks a save until the missing information is entered. You met all of them in the demo above.

    1. Under Review gate

    A case cannot move to Under Review while Missing Information items are still open.

    Validation rule, RecordType = Free_Goods && Sub_Type__c = Product Replacement.

    2. Approval needs a PO

    Recording the manufacturer’s approval requires the PO number and PO date.

    3. Denial needs comments

    Recording a denial requires the manufacturer’s comments.

    4. Delivery needs proof

    Shipment Complete requires PO number, tracking number and shipment date.

    5. One product per site per year

    A site cannot have a new request while another replacement for that site was approved within the last 365 days (counted from the PO date). This is why the PO date is captured.

    Added to PRO_Free_Goods_Fast_Field_Update_Flow for Product Replacement only. Block or warn is decision D4.

    Rules the case inherits from Free Goods (already live)

    Sub Type must be one of the four types, a Person Account is required, and a Cancelled case needs a Sub Status. The "HIPAA Valid at creation" rule is exempted for Product Replacement (decided 11/05): a missing HIPAA becomes a Missing Information item instead. The first program has the money-related switches (income check, leadership approval, patient acceptance, renewals, and so on) turned off, so the eligibility and financial automations stay quiet for this case without any change to them.

    What this design deliberately does not do

    No separate request object

    One request, one decision, one shipment, so one case is enough. Same conclusion as July.

    No automated integrations

    No manufacturer connection, email automation or courier link in Phase 1. Submission stays a manual Outlook email.

    No change for other programs

    PAP, Quick Start and Bridge keep working as they do today. The only touch is one extra condition on the document-check automation and a Product Replacement exemption on the HIPAA creation rule.

    No provider-level replacement

    A site's own allowance (one vial per period with no named patient) is a different business rule and is out of scope. This design supports replacement for an enrolled patient.

    No migration of the first program

    Moving the first program off the older system is a separate project, and it is the reason eligibility is confirmed by hand.

    Plain-English glossary

    Case
    One record that tracks a single request from start to finish.
    Free Goods
    The type of case used when a manufacturer gives product at no charge. Sub-types: PAP, Quick Start, Bridge and Product Replacement.
    Sub Type
    Which kind of Free Goods request this is. Here it is always Product Replacement.
    Status and Sub Status
    The big stage (such as Under Review) and the detail inside it (such as Pending Determination). Product Replacement uses only the values Free Goods already has.
    To-do (task)
    A piece of work assigned to a person with a due date. Here four to-dos, created one after the other, show where the request is.
    Closed status
    A stage that locks the case as finished. Approved and Denied are closed in this org.
    Validation rule
    A rule that refuses to save a record until required information is entered.
    Flow
    An automation that does steps for people, like stamping dates or creating to-dos.
    Attested
    Confirmed by a person ticking a box, rather than worked out by the system.
    Missing Information
    The existing checklist feature that tracks outstanding items on a case.
    Person Account
    A Salesforce account that represents an individual, here the patient.
    Treating site
    The facility where the patient is treated and where the replacement kit is delivered.
    Dynamic Forms
    Page sections that show or hide depending on the record, so Product Replacement fields appear only when relevant.
    PO (purchase order)
    The order number for the replacement kit. Its date starts the 12-month site clock.
    HIPAA Valid
    A flag showing the patient's privacy authorization is in good order.
    Program switches
    Settings on the program record that turn financial checks on or off. All are off for the manufacturer.
