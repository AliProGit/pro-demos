Four systems, one direction

CareTend is the pharmacy system. Nobody in Salesforce types pharmacy data any more: it is copied from CareTend's own tables into the prescription record, every 30 minutes, and Salesforce decides which patient and program it belongs to.

One rule to remember: MuleSoft copies, Salesforce decides. The review flag, the reason, the program and the patient link are never sent by MuleSoft; the Salesforce trigger sets them when the row lands.

Follow one prescription

A pharmacist completes a fill in CareTend. Press Play and watch the same prescription move through the four systems and land in the Pharmacy Details section.

Steps 4 and 5 are where the questions come: "why is Origin Code a sentence" (restricted picklist, Mule translates the digit), "what if CareTend sends the same Rx twice" (upsert on Rx id, update not duplicate). Step 6 is the Salesforce trigger from TD-2052, unchanged by this work.
